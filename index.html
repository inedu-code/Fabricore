The Predictive Maintenance AI Stack

Four AI layers that transform machine sounds into actionable maintenance intelligence.

Acoustic Anomaly Detection

Deep learning models trained on 40M+ hours of machine audio detect subtle changes in sound signatures — catching spindle imbalances and bearing wear before they escalate.

Acoustic AI

Vibration Frequency Analysis

High-sensitivity accelerometers capture microscopic vibrations. FFT and wavelet transforms identify specific fault frequencies — matching them to known failure modes.

FFT • Wavelet

Failure Prediction Engine

Recurrent neural networks analyze time-series vibration data to predict remaining useful life (RUL) for bearings, belts, spindles, and motors — with days or weeks of lead time.

RNN • RUL

Real-Time Alert System

When anomalies are detected, maintenance teams receive instant alerts via SMS, email, and dashboard — with severity scores, location, and recommended action.
